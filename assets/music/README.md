## How to pick

1. **Luxury wristwatch ad (online):** for slow close-ups, light sweeps and ticking hands, start with *Silent Descent* or *Skyline*. Cut on the swells and keep a short silence before the logo. For a modern, fashion-style edit with quick product turns, use *Deep Techno Ambience* and cut on the kick. All three are fine for Facebook/Instagram/YouTube **paid ads**. If the same ad will also run on **TV, cinema or radio**, do not use any Mixkit track. Use a CC0 track or buy a broadcast licence.
2. **Bengali educational and topic videos (long-form, voice-led):** use `calm-education/`. Pick *Lo-Fi 04* or *Serene View* for the steadiest bed. Pick *Easy Going Music Loop* when you want a track with no restrictions at all (CC0). For longer videos, rotate tracks per chapter, or loop with a 2–3 s crossfade, rather than letting one track repeat for 10 minutes.
3. **Product/explainer with a positive tone:** use `corporate-upbeat/`. *Your Breath* is long enough for a 3–4 minute explainer without looping.
4. **Tech/screen-recording:** use `tech-modern/`. Both tracks have few melodic hooks, so they don't fight the narration.
5. **Reels/Shorts (≤ 60–90 s):** use `energetic-reels/`. Start the music on the first frame, put the hook on a downbeat, and use the BPM to set cut lengths (at 120 BPM one beat = 0.5 s, one bar = 2 s). Confirm the beat grid before relying on it.
6. **Emotional story:** use `emotional-story/`. Line up the track's build with the story's turning point. *Dreaming of You* is mastered very quietly (-25.5 LUFS), so raise it, don't judge it by its raw level.

### Mixing under speech
- Aim for the finished video at about **-14 LUFS** (YouTube/Facebook/Instagram normalise around there), with the **voice clearly on top**. Under continuous speech the music bed should sit roughly **18–25 dB below the voice**. Raise it in the pauses and at the intro and outro (ducking).
- The source files range from -7 to -25.5 LUFS, so **never use them at 100 %**: set each one's level in the edit.
- Trimming, fading, looping and ducking inside your own video are fine under both licences. Do not make a "remix" or a music-only track out of Mixkit music.
- The `ffmpeg-skill` (background music with ducking, loudness normalisation) can do the bed and the duck for you.

## Rules for the editor (read before every upload)

1. **Never register any of these tracks with Content ID, Facebook Rights Manager or any other rights-management/fingerprinting service.** Don't register your videos that contain them either, unless the music has been removed from what you register. The Mixkit licence explicitly forbids it ("register them on any rights management service"). For the CC0 tracks you hold no exclusive rights, so registering would break YouTube's and Meta's rules and would hit every other creator using them.
2. **Don't redistribute.** Keep this folder private. Don't upload the MP3s on their own, give them to other channels, or ship them inside templates or packs (Mixkit User Terms, clause 9).
3. **Mixkit = online only.** YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, web and online ads: yes. TV, radio, cinema, DVD/CD, video games: no.
4. **If a video gets a claim** (sometimes a third party wrongly registers a free track):
   - Mixkit track: dispute it in YouTube Studio ("I have a license"), quoting the licence URL and the track details from `LICENSES.md`, and forward the claim to **team@mixkit.co** as the licence asks.
   - CC0 track: dispute it, quoting the Freesound page URL and "CC0 1.0 Universal / public domain".
   - Keep `LICENSES.md` and these exact files. They are your proof of source.
5. **Credits are optional** for all 16 tracks. If you want to credit anyway (for example in a YouTube description), copy the exact credit line from `LICENSES.md`.
6. **Adding a track later:** open the source's licence page the same day, confirm your use is allowed, download only from the official site, and add a row to `LICENSES.md` (file, title, artist, source page, licence + URL, date, uses, credit). No CC-BY-NC, no "personal use only", nothing ripped from YouTube or other sites.

### Pixabay Music: use with care
Licence: Pixabay Content License (https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/). It is free and needs no attribution. Standalone redistribution is not allowed, and you may not use content showing recognisable brands commercially. Pixabay's FAQ (https://pixabay.com/service/faq/) says some contributors register their tracks with Content ID. Those tracks carry a **"Content ID Registered"** badge on their page and "may trigger claims" even when used legally. On YouTube you can dispute such a claim with the track's licence certificate. Facebook and Instagram may mute or block the video automatically.

**Of 21 Pixabay track pages opened on 2026-10-18, 18 carried the "Content ID Registered" badge.** That included tracks literally titled "No Copyright Music" and the popular "Lofi Study" by FASSounds. Rule: **skip any Pixabay track with that badge, especially for paid ads.**

### YouTube Audio Library (YouTube Studio, login)
Help page checked today: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3376882. It says you can monetise videos that use Audio Library music, and downloaded tracks "won't be claimed by a rights holder through the Content ID system". The page does **not** say whether the music may be used on Facebook or Instagram or in ads. **Treat Audio Library music as YouTube-only** unless the terms shown inside Studio say otherwise.

Individual Audio Library tracks have no public links, so here are filters to use instead:
- Studio, then **Audio library**, then filter **License type: "Attribution not required"** (or copy the exact Creative Commons credit into the description if a track requires it).
- Suggested searches: *Mood: Calm / Genre: Ambient or Classical* (education), *Mood: Bright / Genre: Pop or Dance & Electronic* (corporate and Shorts), *Mood: Dramatic or Inspirational / Genre: Cinematic* (luxury and emotional).
